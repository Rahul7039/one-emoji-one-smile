# One Emoji, One Smile — candidate emojis

Judge brief (in priority order): (1) a single emoji a stranger reads as warm and
friendly, most likely to make someone smile; (2) warmth at first glance, no context
needed; (3) universality across age/background, no irony or in-joke; (4) chosen, not
the first smiley that comes to mind, while staying instantly readable; (5) right for
a stranger — no intimacy, sarcasm or flirtation. Novelty alone does not count.

## Candidates

1. 😁 Beaming Face with Smiling Eyes — **CHOSEN.** A big, toothy, eyes-smiling grin:
   the most infectious warmth at first glance, instantly readable in every culture,
   joyful without a trace of intimacy or irony, and a deliberate step past the plain
   default smileys. Wins criteria 1–2 outright and holds up on 3–5.
2. 😄 Grinning Face with Smiling Eyes — Near-twin of the chosen grin, but the smile
   reads a touch less exuberant at a glance, so it loses to 😁 on criterion 2.
3. 😊 Smiling Face with Smiling Eyes — Warm and universal, but the single most
   default "nice" smiley; loses on criterion 4 to any equally warm, more chosen face.
4. 🥰 Smiling Face with Hearts — Very warm, but the hearts signal affection; from a
   stranger that can read as flirtatious, so it fails criterion 5.
5. 🤗 Hugging Face — Warm and friendly, but a hug from someone unknown can read as
   over-familiar, and the gesture is less instantly readable than a plain grin.
6. 🌞 Sun with Face — Cheerful, distinctive and universal with no intimacy, but its
   warmth is metaphorical; a human grin aimed at you beats it on criteria 1–2.
7. 🐣 Hatching Chick — Cute and reliably smile-inducing, but cute is not warm and
   friendly, and from a stranger it reads random rather than warm — loses on 1.

## Verdict

😁 — the beaming grin. It is the warmest face a stranger can meet at a glance, with
nothing in it that could land wrong.
